ShoeyCat is a deadcat-style, 3D-printable micro quad frame in multiple sizes. 

**Recommended Spec**

*ShoeyCat 1.6*
- 40mm Props
- 0802 motors (as much KV as you like)
- BetaFPV Matrix II 5-in-1 AIO
- BetaFPV C03 camera + Air canopy
- 1S 550-650mAh battery

*ShoeyCat 1.8*
- 45mm Props
- 1102 motors (ensure 9x9mm mounting)
- BetaFPV Matrix II 5-in-1 AIO
- BetaFPV C03 camera + Air canopy
- 1s 650-850mAh battery

**Recommended Print Settings**
- Print in CF nylon (*e.g.*, PA6-CF, PAHT-CF) or PC-CF (I have tested PPS-CF as well and it performed worse than either of these materials, sadge)
- Walls: minimum 5
- Top/Bottom:  minimum 5
- Infill: minimum 50%
- Other tips: print hot, slow, and with minimal fan for maximum strength and rigidity; dry your filament (ESPECIALLY nylon)
